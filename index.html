FAQ

QUESTIONS
we get A LOT.

Do you do free quotes?

Yes. Send a few photos to our number and we can give you a ballpark. For a fixed-price quote we'll come out and measure — no charge across greater Sydney.

How long does a house take?

A standard single-storey is usually 4–6 working days including curing time between coats. Two-storey homes, weather and access can extend that. We give you a clear timeline before we start.

Can you render over brick / blockwork / besser?

Yes — brick, block, AAC, Hebel, besser and existing render are all surfaces we work with regularly. We assess and prep each substrate so the new render bonds properly.

Cement render vs. acrylic texture — which one?

Traditional cement render is the base coat that bonds to the wall. Acrylic texture is a flexible, UV-stable topcoat. Most exteriors get both. We'll walk you through what suits your job.

Do you work with builders?

Plenty. We slot into new-build schedules and can coordinate with your site supervisor. We invoice through standard progress claims.

How far do you travel?

Across greater Sydney metro. Outside that, give us a call — bigger jobs we'll travel for.
