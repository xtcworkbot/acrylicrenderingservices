FAQ

Questions
We Get A Lot.

Do you do free quotes?

Yes. Send a few photos and we can give you a ballpark. For a fixed-price quote we'll come out and measure — no charge across greater Sydney.

What's the difference between sand cement and acrylic?

Sand cement is the traditional base-coat render that bonds to your wall. Acrylic is a flexible, UV-stable topcoat with deep colour life. Most exteriors get both — we'll walk you through what suits.

What is microcement and where does it go?

Microcement is an ultra-thin (2–3mm) cement coating for interior surfaces — floors, walls, vanities, joinery. Seamless, water-resistant and finished glass-smooth. Modern alternative to tile.

Can you render over brick, block or besser?

Yes — brick, block, AAC, Hebel, besser and existing render are all surfaces we work with regularly. We assess and prep each substrate so the new render bonds properly.

How long does a house take?

A standard single-storey is usually 4–6 working days including curing time between coats. Two-storey, weather and access can extend that. We give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do you work with builders?

Plenty. We slot into new-build schedules and coordinate with your site supervisor. We invoice through standard progress claims.
